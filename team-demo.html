Match scores measure facial similarity, not accuracy or identity certainty. Unknown means no confident match to these four teammates. Camera frames are processed transiently and are not stored.

Presentation roleplay

  1. Anthony and Leo enter together: show their names, green boxes and match scores.
  2. Enable the Olivia simulation, then have Olivia enter. Her box turns amber and displays Unknown.
  3. A teammate plays reception staff: "I could not verify this participant from the demo gallery. May I check your visitor pass?"
  4. Explain: Unknown means an unmatched face, not a threat. This demo does not send notifications.
  5. Turn off the simulation to show Olivia recognized again. Use the separate check-in page to demonstrate attendance.
